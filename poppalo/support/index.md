## How to play

Wolves float down on balloons to steal Mama Pig's piglets. **Drag** to move
Mama Pig up and down the lift and **tap** to sling an acorn. Pop every balloon
on a wolf before it lands; if one grabs a piglet, pop it on the way up and the
piglet parachutes home. Lose every piglet and the run ends.

## Purchases

- **Remove Ads** removes the ads shown between games. Optional ads you choose
  to watch (for a second chance or new blessing cards) are still available.
- **Supporter Pack** removes those ads too, and gives you second chances and
  new blessing cards without watching an ad.

Both are one-time purchases. **New phone or reinstalled the game?** Open
*Settings › Restore purchases* in the game while signed in with the same Apple
ID (or Google account) you bought with.

Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone and iPad, use
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Ads and privacy

- *Settings › Personalized ads* in the game turns ads picked for you on or off.
- On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking for Poppalo in
  *iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- *Settings › Sound* mutes the game.

The game has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device, so
deleting the game also deletes your best score. Full details are in the
[privacy policy](/poppalo/privacy/).
