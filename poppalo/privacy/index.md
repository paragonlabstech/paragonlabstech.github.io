*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

Poppalo: Balloon Pop 3D ("the game") is made by Paragon Labs ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.
Questions: paragonlabstech@outlook.com.

## What the game keeps on your device

The game saves a small file on your device with your best score, your sound
setting, which purchases you own, your ad-personalization choice, and simple
counters (how many times you have played). It stays on your device; we do not
receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and
the game has no accounts.

## Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: optional rewarded videos (for example, to get a
second chance) and occasional ads between games. Ads are provided by **Unity
LevelPlay** and **Unity Ads**, operated by Unity Technologies.

To serve and measure ads, these services collect and process, from your device:

- your device's advertising identifier (and similar device IDs);
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- how you interact with the ads (views, taps);
- technical and diagnostic information about the app and device.

They use it to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service
secure, and, **only if you allow it**, to show ads based on your interests.

**Your choice.** The first time you play, the game asks whether you allow
personalized ads, and you can change your answer any time under
*Settings › Personalized ads*. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are
not based on your interests. On iPhone and iPad, the game also asks for Apple's
tracking permission; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.
You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Unity handles this data, and how to request access or deletion:
- Unity Privacy Hub (covers Unity Ads and LevelPlay): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Unity "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information

## Purchases

You can buy "Remove Ads" or the "Supporter Pack". Payment is handled entirely
by Google Play or Apple's App Store under their own privacy policies; we never
see your payment details. The store tells the game which items you own so it
can unlock them, including when you restore purchases on a new device.

## Analytics

The game does not currently send gameplay analytics to us or anyone else.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If
you believe a child has used the game and want data removed from an ad
partner, contact us at paragonlabstech@outlook.com and use the Unity links above.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the
CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to
the processing of personal data. Because we don't hold your data ourselves,
requests about ad data are handled by Unity through the links above; we're
happy to help at paragonlabstech@outlook.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-game link.
